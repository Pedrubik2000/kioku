Kioku runs the open-source Anki desktop app (26.08) natively on iPhone and iPad: the deck list, reviewer, card browser, editor, statistics, deck options and desktop add-ons, adapted for touch.

Please test with a copy of your collection (sync from AnkiWeb, or import a .colpkg through the Files app), and back up first: this is early software.

What to try: reviewing, Browse, adding and editing notes, statistics, deck options, AnkiWeb sync, and the add-ons you use. Known limits: ogg/opus audio does not play yet; add-ons that start other programs (such as MeCab readings) cannot work on iOS.

Report problems at https://github.com/krmanik/kioku/issues or with TestFlight's feedback.

Kioku is an independent open-source project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Ankitects.
