Kioku runs the open-source Anki desktop app (26.08) natively on iPhone and iPad. It is the same program you use on your computer, not a look-alike: the same deck list, reviewer, card browser, editor, statistics and deck options, adapted for a touch screen.

DESKTOP FEATURES IN YOUR POCKET
• The full card browser: search, sort, edit, find and replace, change note types.
• The editor with rich text, images, audio and image occlusion.
• The new statistics page with charts, the calendar and future due forecast.
• Deck options with FSRS, filtered decks, custom study and note type editing.
• Import and export .apkg and .colpkg files through the Files app.

ADD-ONS
Install desktop add-ons from AnkiWeb inside the app with their codes. Popular add-ons such as AnkiConnect, Review Heatmap, HyperTTS, Advanced Browser and Edit Field During Review have been tested. Add-ons that start other programs cannot work on iOS.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
Your collection lives on your device, in Kioku's folder in the Files app. Kioku collects no data and has no analytics or ads. Sync with your own AnkiWeb account, or move collections with exported files.

MADE FOR TOUCH
Every window opens full screen with a Back button, dialogs stay above the keyboard, and the layout fits portrait and landscape.
